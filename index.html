Hi, you've found me...

I write software.

Usually that means starting somewhere on the surface and eventually finding myself underneath it — figuring out the APIs, the data, the integrations, the infrastructure, and all the little things that make the whole system work.

That's the part I enjoy.

A lot of software doesn't live in one place.

A message starts in WhatsApp.
A payment happens in M-Pesa.
An API carries the request.
An AI agent finds the right information.
A CRM records what happened.

I like being in the middle of that.

Connecting the pieces, deciding how they should communicate, and turning separate systems into something that works as one.

I Love What I Do

And this is how I do what I do.

I bite into complex systems in small pieces.

Think about what talks to what, where the data goes, what happens when something fails, and how the system changes when ten users become ten thousand.

I like understanding the individual pieces, putting them together, and seeing the whole thing come alive.
